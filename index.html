Frequently Asked Questions

What are Bitcoin transaction fees?

Bitcoin transaction fees are payments included with transactions so miners can prioritize and confirm them in a block. Transaction fees and block rewards are the incentives for miners to mine the blockchain.

What is the next-block fee?

The next-block fee is an estimate of the fee rate that may help a transaction confirm in the next available block.

What is the median Bitcoin fee?

The median fee represents the middle fee value among a selected group of recent transactions.

How often are the fee estimates updated?

BitcoinFees.Live updates its displayed fee data approximately every two minutes.

Are these fee estimates guaranteed?

No. Fee estimates are probabilistic and can change as network conditions change. Always review the fee recommended by your wallet before sending.
